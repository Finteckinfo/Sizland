# EU Ecosystem Application Pack (Free-First Template)

Copy/paste sections from here when applying to EUSPA/ESA programmes or when emailing potential EU partners.

## 1) One-line pitch
Sizland is a verification-driven land acquisition workflow that uses Copernicus EO evidence and auditable due-diligence documentation to reduce fraud and enable cross-border land investment across Africa.

## 2) Problem
Remote land acquisition in many African markets has a “trust deficit”: inconsistent verification, high fraud risk, and limited transparency for cross-border buyers.

## 3) Solution (what we built)
- Copernicus Sentinel-2 EO evidence retrieval and persistence, shown as “Satellite-Verified” plots
- Land request workflow with progress milestones
- Due diligence evidence submission (docs-only in MVP-minimum) and workflow status tracking
- Escrow milestone tracking (escrow identifiers/status recorded)

## 4) Why EU Space Programme matters
Copernicus provides an independent, sovereign EU data source that strengthens verification and investor confidence. We operationalize EO evidence inside a transactional workflow, not just as a visualization layer.

## 5) Pilot and scaling plan
- Pilot: Kenya (operational playbook + partners)
- Rollout: expand to additional African markets by standardizing evidence checklists and onboarding local due-diligence partners

## 6) What we are asking for (choose as applicable)
- Matchmaking to EU corporates/investors/partners
- Technical mentorship on EO verification productization and evidence standards
- Support connecting to additional EU data providers and commercial imagery options

## 7) Evidence pack checklist (attachments)
- Screenshots of “Satellite-Verified” plot selection
- Admin due diligence document upload + status set to `DUE_DILIGENCE`
- Short demo video (optional)
- KPIs: number of requests created, plots verified, documents uploaded, pilot outcomes

## 8) Intervention needed (human)
Maintain a folder of receipts and confirmations:
- Submission receipts (CASSINI matchmaking, ESA BIC, etc.)
- Acceptance/rejection letters or emails
- Partner introductions and meeting notes (date, org, outcome)
